# Join Teams call with Meeting ID and passcode

One way of joining a Teams call is with a link that you typically receive by e-mail.

If you don't have e-mail yet or can't access it, another way to join a Teams call is with two pieces of information: a so-called Meeting ID and passcode.

Here is how to do that:
- open Teams
- on the left edge of Teams, click on the camera icon for which the (english) tooltip says `Meet`
- on the right side of Teams, click on the button `Join with a meeting ID`
- in the dialog that pops up, enter the Meeting ID and the passcode you have received by messenger pigeon (or some other means of transport)
- click `Join meeting`
