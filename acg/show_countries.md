## Quick guides

The Country Contacts Page is pretty easy to use. But if you need help, you can follow these guides:

<details>
<summary>How You Can Use and Change the Page</summary>

- 🦉Note that this page does not store any information on your computer or on a server. The information is only stored in the link.
- 👁️ in the `Countries to show` panel at the top, on the right side of the `Region` dropdown menu, notice the arrow pointing to the right
- 🔨 keep on clicking on this Regions arrow and notice how the 18 global subregions get selected and their countries are shown
- 🔨 go to the subregion "Northern Europe" and notice that there are countries with and without contacts
- 🔨 click on the country `Denmark` and in the panel that opens, notice the population info and "Contacts: indirect" and then click on the green `indirect` button
- 👁️ notice that the panel now says "Contacts: none" and that on the page, the number under the small `Denmark` flag disappeared
- 🔨 click on `indirect` again and notice that the number under `Denmark` reappeared
- 👁️ in the "Countries to show" panel, notice the three buttons labeled "direct", "indirect" and "none" next to "Contacts" - these are for filtering countries where we have direct, indirect or no contacts
- 🔨 click on the green "indirect" button to deselect it, leaving the "direct" button active
- 👁️ notice how the flags with a number now all show a 1, showing the countries where we only have direct contacts
- 🔨 click on the "indirect" button again so that all contact info is shown

</details>
