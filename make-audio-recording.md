# How to make an audio recording

## Preparation
- go to a quiet room without much echo: living room or bedroom rather than kitchen
- on iPhone or iPad, open the app Voice Memo
- the microphone of the device should be 20-30 cm from your mouth, ideally a few cm below it
- rotate the device just by a few degrees, so that the mic is not pointing directly at your mouth
- place the device on a stable surface so that you don't have to hold it

## Recording
- try to relax and remember that people need to be able to understand you - so try to speak slowly and clearly
- do a test recording, check whether it is ok and adjust if necessary
- start another recording, wait 1-2 seconds, say the message a few times, directly one after the other, making a pause of 1-2 seconds between them
- listen to the recording and if ok, click on `Share` to send it
